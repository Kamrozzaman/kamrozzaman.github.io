abstract: >-
  The first systematic benchmark of Bengali deepfake audio detection on BanglaFake. Zero-shot inference with
  Wav2Vec2-XLSR, Whisper, WavLM, PANNs and AST does poorly, while fine-tuned models
  (Wav2Vec2, LCNN, ResNet18, ViT, CNN-BiLSTM) do much better.
