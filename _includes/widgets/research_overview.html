Research & Skills

My research focuses on generative models and on detecting AI-generated content, with a particular interest in Bengali and other low-resource settings. Recent work covers:

  • Generation: GAN-based Bengali handwritten word synthesis from plain text (BeHGAN).
  • Image forensics: fusing FFT spectral cues with CLIP semantics to detect AI-generated images (FUSE).
  • Audio forensics: benchmarking zero-shot and fine-tuned models for Bengali deepfake speech.
  • Text forensics & NLP: detecting LLM-paraphrased Bengali text, and class imbalance in Bengali review rating prediction.
  • Transfer learning for tabular data: transformers and LLM fine-tuning for transfer learning between tabular data sets from different domains that share no features, by transferring attention (key/value projection) weights from a pretrained transformer or fine-tuned LLM into a tabular transformer.
